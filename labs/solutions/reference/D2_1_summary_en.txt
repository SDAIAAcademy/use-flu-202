In September 2026 the shared-service centre received 29 written inquiries from staff of the Marjan Coastal Tourism Authority, after duplicates were removed. The most frequent topics were delayed requests (7), leave and attendance (5) and portal sign-in difficulties (5). The average processing time of the 19 closed requests was 7.4 working days. The centre proposed a short meeting with the Authority's team after the self-service portal launches on 15 November.
